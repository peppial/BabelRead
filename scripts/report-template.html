The two numbers are different kinds of claim and must not be added. Recorded is fact — a commit trailer, a bot author, or a provenance note says an agent wrote those lines. Inferred is a guess from code style, and style is weak evidence: a thorough developer, a house lint rule, a formatter in CI, or a squash merge all produce the same signals. A recorded count of zero means no record exists, not that a human wrote it — nobody on this team has provenance capture installed. Use the inferred lane to decide where to look harder in review. Do not use it as an audit record, and do not use it about a person.
